This is one successful physical trial; the software pass and closed outcome are separate evidence.

The change addresses the pre-open admission path after [Run49's failure on R56](revision56_validation.md). That trial reached measurement collection and timed out before opening. Its recorded reason was the last individual sample result, not proof of a completed stationary span or successful final admission. The closed failure analysis found residual arm settling near the nominal observation window, but the observer's sparse samples cannot identify the exact internal veto. Its detailed record is local-only: `output/official_reference/run49_review/preopen_failure_independent_review/README.md`. No contact-generation or physical-motion cause is claimed as established.

The pre-open observation allowance increases from **5 to 10 wall seconds**. It still requires 100 ms of consecutive producer-time stationarity and retains the existing position, velocity, freshness, inter-sample-gap and clock catch-up bounds. Ten seconds is an experimental allowance for settling, not a duration proved sufficient by Run49. No opening is authorized merely because that time has elapsed.

In selected stock-close mode, the gate retains its existing retention and scene checks, then verifies current retained contact sides and ages, master-joint range, fresh finite stock feedback and fault state under the existing command-then-sensor locking order. An ordinary contact-generation advance can qualify only with that current retention proof. Nonstock mode keeps the prior generation-equality retry. This check covers current stock-retention state; it is **not an atomic whole-scene snapshot**. The preceding scene check and the subsequent `_open_gripper` scene check remain in place.

The existing success/failure event gains bounded timing diagnostics: sample and reset counts, the longest observed stationary span, final-admission attempts and veto reasons, generation retries/revalidations and elapsed clocks. These distinguish a last-sample reason from a final-admission refusal. Publication remains outside locks, and a diagnostic publication failure cannot authorize opening. The [source review](evidence/revision57/preopen_source_review.json) records the change's scope.
